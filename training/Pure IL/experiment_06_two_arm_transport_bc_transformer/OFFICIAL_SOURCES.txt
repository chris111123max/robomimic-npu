Official sources used for Experiment 06
=======================================

1. robomimic official Transformer tutorial:
   https://robomimic.github.io/docs/tutorials/training_transformers.html

2. robomimic official tuned BC-Transformer template:
   robomimic/config/default_templates/bc_transformer.json

3. robomimic official low-dimensional experiment generator:
   robomimic/scripts/generate_paper_configs.py

The experiment config combines:
- the official tuned Transformer architecture / optimizer settings;
- the official Transport low-dimensional observation definition;
- the official PH validation split convention;
- the official low-dimensional 50-rollout / 50-epoch evaluation protocol;
- the local dataset and output paths.
